# Scanned hall (iPhone LiDAR)

Built by `python -m ingest_lidar` from an iPhone Pro LiDAR scan taken with the RoomScanner
app in Mesh mode (about 14 x 14 m, curved walls). People who were in the room during the
scan were removed by OWLv2 detection plus LiDAR (19 people), and 4 exit signs were found
on keyframes. The raw scan (mesh.ply and keyframes) stays on the scanning laptop; only the
derived scene.json and display.glb are here. Validator: 0 errors.
