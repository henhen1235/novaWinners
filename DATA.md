# Datasets

No external datasets are downloaded yet. Synthetic plans are generated by `tools/make_synthetic_plan.py` and are safe to commit when they are small PDFs.

| Dataset | License | Commercial use | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CubiCasa5K | Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 | Yes, with attribution | Download script writes into `data/`. Not committed. |
| Modified Swiss Dwellings | CC BY 4.0 | Yes, with attribution | Research floor plans. Not committed. |
| buildingSMART IFC samples | Various open licenses, check each file | Usually yes | Optional IFC path. |

Non-commercial datasets will be flagged here before use. None are in use.
