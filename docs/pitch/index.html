Limits and next
09 / Where it stands

Rehearse the emergency before it happens.

What we do not claim

  • Not a certification. A planning tool for deciding what to fix and test next.
  • Decisions are not yet validated against real evacuations; movement follows standard flow and speed limits.
  • No fire physics. Fire and smoke are scheduled blocking events.
  • Single runs, seed 42. Not a general result.

Next

  • Verify movement on standard test cases: free walking, door flow.
  • Many seeds, with ranges on every number.
  • Live what-if mode: add a fire or a person while you watch.
  • More buildings from floor plans, starting with a dormitory.

Scan or plan in. Drill, break, fix, measure. Find who gets left behind before it is real.
